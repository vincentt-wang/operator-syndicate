Deal Submission

Submit your deal.

A short questionnaire plus your pitch deck. Reviewed personally by senior operators, scored objectively, and matched into the ecosystem.

Why submit to EvoScale

More than capital

EvoScale is an operator-led syndicate. Alongside capital, you gain an operator network and direct access to the enterprise buyers you are trying to reach.

Every deal is evaluated objectively through our D-F-V framework: desirability, feasibility, viability.

What we ask of you

The form

  • About 12 questions, roughly 5 minutes. Your answers save automatically as you go.
  • Referral to the ecosystem or partner VCs follows the consent choices you make at the end.

The deck

  • The final step is uploading a 10 to 15 page pitch deck. Anything the form does not ask, your deck should carry.
  • Your deck must cover the checklist below; each item maps to an evaluation dimension.
