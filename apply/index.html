Why EvoScale

More than capital

EvoScale is an angel network of working senior operators. Beyond the check, members bring industry experience and relationships that can help you reach enterprise customers.

We look at three things: does someone really need this (Desirability), can you build and deliver it (Feasibility), and can it become a lasting business (Viability).

How it works: after we read your materials, we set up a 30-minute call. If it goes well, we invite you to meet our members at the monthly Deal Room, and from there to the investment committee.
